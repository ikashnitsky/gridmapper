# gridmapper (development version)

* Initial R port of the 'gridmapper' JavaScript library: `allocate_grid()`,
  `allocate_grid_simple()`, `estimate_parameters()`,
  `estimate_parameters_from_data()`, `grid_dimensions()`, `compute_spacers()`,
  `pca_angle()`, `pca_rotate()`, `polygon_centroid()`, `adjacency_graph()`,
  `embed_graph()`, `grid_to_sf()`, `dorling_cartogram()` and
  `demers_cartogram()`.
